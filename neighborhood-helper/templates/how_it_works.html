How Neighborhood Helper Works

A safe, transparent, and direct local service platform connecting residents with trusted neighborhood helpers.

The End-to-End Booking Lifecycle

1

1. Search Local Helpers by City & Area

Select your city (e.g. Coimbatore or Chennai) and choose your specific neighborhood. Accurate Haversine calculations guarantee you see nearby local helpers.

2

2. Select Helper & Book Service

Compare verified helpers by rating, distance, experience, and customer reviews. Select your preferred date, time, and service location to book with a unique Booking ID.

3

3. Helper Accepts Request

The assigned local helper reviews the request details and accepts the booking. Both you and the helper have access to direct contact details.

4

4. Helper Arrives & Completes Work

The helper arrives at your location, marks work "In Progress", solves the issue, and marks the service "Completed" with zero complex checkout hurdles.

5

5. User Submits Rating & Review

After work completion, provide your 1 to 5 star rating and feedback comment. This dynamically calculates and updates the helper's neighborhood reputation score.
